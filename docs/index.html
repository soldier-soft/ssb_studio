The Three Core Psychological Tests

Comprehensive simulation covering all three written batteries conducted on Day 2 of SSB Interviews.

⚡

Word Association Test (WAT)

60 consecutive words flashed for 15 seconds each. Candidates must write their spontaneous thought without hesitation.

  • Pop-in word reveal animations
  • Rapid response recording & autosave
  • Practice Mode: navigate back & forth
  • 60 standard words pre-loaded in sample bank
🎯

Situation Reaction Test (SRT)

60 real-life day-to-day, social, and military crisis situations to evaluate promptness and practical officer qualities.

  • High-contrast highlight on reaction stem '( ... )'
  • Word & character density live feedback
  • 30-second per slide or 30-minute full exam
  • 60 standard situations pre-loaded
🖼️

Thematic Apperception Test (TAT)

12 pictures shown consecutively. Candidates write structured stories describing the past, present, feelings, and positive resolution.

  • 30 seconds picture viewing phase
  • 4 minutes (270s) story writing phase
  • Picture #12 Blank Slide handling
  • Word count tracking & evaluator story notes
